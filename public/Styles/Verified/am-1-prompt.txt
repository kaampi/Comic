Illustration style:
Polished contemporary digital comic, precise visible ink, clean digital color and restrained illustrated volume. Naturalistically stylized adults and expressive drawn faces, not manga, photography or CGI. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, actions, emotions, objects, relative scale, setting and time period. Expressions follow the scene; no forced action, costumes or smiles.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Confident dark silhouette contours with controlled thick-to-thin variation, selected medium structural lines and few fine facial details. Hair broad organized locks, clothes a few clear folds. Animals, machines and environment equally drawn. Machines large simple outlined masses, simple joints, few seams and selected identifying features. No intricate mechanical parts.

Color and shading:
Rich restrained local colors in large completely smooth areas. Each major form has one uniform base color, one broad designed shadow mass and optional small highlight accent. One restrained soft transition on a few focal forms for subtle volume; elsewhere clean drawn tonal boundaries. No mottled pigment, texture brushes, cloudy variation, grain, realistic reflections or lit bevels. Preserve specified colors and lighting.

Texture and finish:
Polish through drawing and tonal organization, not material detail. Large smooth uninterrupted surfaces. When specified, rust and moss a few large single asymmetrical flat color patches with plain interiors and gently uneven outlines, no symbols, fragments, dots, scratches, cracks or strands. Forest few huge overlapping canopy masses with broad tonal shapes, empty interiors, sparse contour lobes. Trunks plain. Ground broad open color plane with few grass clumps and smooth outlined rocks, no pebbles or grass carpet. Fields broad bands, sparse furrows. Water smooth ribbons, selected glints and drawn ripples. Patterns sparse broad grids or few motifs. No photographic underpainting, grain, pores, fine hair or wood grain. Camera and effects separate.
