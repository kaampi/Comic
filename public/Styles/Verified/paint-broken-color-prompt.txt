Illustration style:
Expressive naturalistic broken-color painting with fewer larger groups of distinct unblended brushstrokes. Same broad scale on human faces, animals, machines and landscape. Large coherent value groups preserve narrative clarity. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, poses, emotions, objects, scale, setting, time of day and light sources.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Broad directional paint marks with no ink contours. Faces three or four adjacent planes and selected feature accents, age through silhouette/value, no fine portrait rendering. Hands grouped strokes, hair broad masses, clothes a few large directional groups, sparse painted pattern suggestions. Animals correct anatomy, head and body broad painted planes, no fine fur or tack. Machines big structural masses, simple joints, sparse identifying bands.

Color and shading:
Warm ochre/earth red and cool blue/violet/green strokes sit side by side within forms, mixing optically. Group separate strokes into coherent light/shadow masses, not smooth gradients or a uniform mosaic of tiny dabs. Large calm painted areas alternate with lively accents. Preserve requested local colors and lighting without imposed sunset or saturation. Wear few broad irregular painted accents.

Texture and finish:
Physical unblended brush edges and moderate pigment body, no random grain or uniform decorative impasto. Canopies few huge stroke groups with broad warm/cool marks, trunks directional strokes. Ground broad calm earth-color groups with few grass/rock groups. Rocks few painted planes, fields long bands, water broad horizontal ribbons and selected light accents. No fine drawing, skin pores, hair strands, tiny leaves, pebbles, corrosion dots, dense prints, photographic underpainting or airbrush. Camera and effects separate.
