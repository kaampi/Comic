Illustration style:
Economical expressive ink brush with thin selected mineral-color layers on luminous pale paper. At least half of image unpainted paper, including open subject interiors. Same sparse broad brush scale throughout. Medium reference only, not a change to cultural setting or subject matter. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, poses, emotions, objects, scale, setting, time of day and light sources. Do not introduce unrelated clothing, architecture or landscape motifs.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Few deliberate variable-pressure dark brush strokes, pressed beginnings, tapered endings, selected dry gaps and broad pale wash passages. Faces few essential accents, age through silhouette and value, no fine portrait details. Hair brush masses, hands simple shapes, clothes few rhythmic folds and sparse pattern hints. Animals natural anatomy, normal small eyes and long muzzle, broad mane/limb gestures and essential tack. Machines broad open masses, sparse dark structural strokes, simple joints and broad identifying bands.

Color and shading:
Restrained muted azurite-like blue, malachite-like green, ochre, earth red and brown accents compatible with requested local colors. Broad controlled ink values and thin selected matte mineral-pigment fields, a few more opaque focal accents. Substantial visible paper; no full-color realistic modeling, airbrush or dense watercolor mottling. Preserve lighting and time of day. Wear only few broad pale irregular color accents.

Texture and finish:
Brush rhythm and negative space primary; subtle paper character secondary. No all-over grain, dots, scratches, filaments, fine leaves, grass carpets, pebbles, dense cloth prints or detailed wood grain. Woodland few broad canopy washes and long trunk strokes with large gaps. Ground largely blank paper, pale path gesture and few grass/rock groups. Fields sparse long strokes, houses few structural forms, water few pale flowing strokes with paper highlights. No photographic underpainting, hatching, decorative writing, calligraphy, seals or stamps. Camera and effects separate.
