Illustration style:
Two-ink risograph stencil print with broad transparent overprints and selected small registration offsets. Naturalistically simplified drawn people and animals, same degree of stylization throughout. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, poses, emotions, objects, scale, setting, time of day and light sources. Translate requested local colors into two-ink separations.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Economical clear contours, broad structural silhouettes, few interior lines. Naturally proportioned heads, readable adult/elderly differences, simple hands and grouped hair. Animals retain natural skull, normal small eyes, muzzle and limb proportions, not cartoon exaggeration. Machines few monumental armor masses, simple joints and broad identifying features. Garments few folds, sparse pattern lines, no detailed structure.

Color and shading:
Exactly two transparent spot inks: deep indigo and warm coral on pale warm-white paper. Broad overlaps produce flat purple mixed shapes. No full-color process palette, gradients or realistic modeling. Small consistent registration offsets on selected major edges create narrow colored fringes and paper slivers; faces remain aligned. Not glow, blur or double anatomy. Requested lighting conveyed by ink/paper design.

Texture and finish:
Prioritize large calm uniform ink fields and clean unprinted paper. Mild stencil ink character secondary to overprint and registration; no uniform heavy noise overlay. Wear only a few broad irregular asymmetrical stain areas, smooth interiors, no botanical identity, symbols, circles, fragments, scratches or tiny plants. Forest few broad overlapping canopy masses, plain trunks, empty interiors. Ground broad open paper with few plant/rock silhouettes and color bands. Water smooth ink ribbons and paper ripples. No fine fur, wood grain, pebble scatter, dense grass, garment microprint or photographic underdrawing. Camera and effects separate.
