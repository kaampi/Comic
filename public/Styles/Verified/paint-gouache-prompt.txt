Illustration style:
Deliberately simplified opaque matte gouache illustration, few large painted shapes and broad brush scale shared by people, animals, machines and scenery. No ink outlines or detailed realistic underpainting. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, poses, emotions, objects, scale, setting, time of day and light sources.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Forms separated by adjacent painted colors, selected sharper edges, broken broad brush boundaries and few dry-brush skips. Faces a few broad planes plus essential feature dabs, clear ages. Hands grouped masses, hair two broad shapes, clothes two or three planes and broad folds. Patterns a few broad paint marks. Animals natural proportions, normal eyes and muzzle, broad painted masses rather than hair or fine tack. Machines monumental planes, simple joints, broad identifying bands, no fine seams.

Color and shading:
Velvety dense matte opaque pigment, two or three broad color/value planes per major form. Harmonious muted local colors and restrained warm-cool shifts. No continuous realistic shading, metal reflections, gloss, airbrush or transparent watercolor staining. Preserve specified colors and light. Rust and moss only few large opaque irregular ochre/olive brush-shapes, no fragments or symbols.

Texture and finish:
Tangible pigment through broad brush edges and selected layered strokes, not grain overlay or tiny dabs. Large calm areas throughout. Canopies huge overlapping paint masses, no little leaves. Trunks broad strokes; ground broad earth-color shapes with few grass/rock accents. Rocks two painted planes, fields broad bands, water broad blue/cream ribbons and selected light strokes. No fine ink, skin pores, hairs, speckling, strands, dense fabric motifs or realistic wood grain. Camera and effects separate.
