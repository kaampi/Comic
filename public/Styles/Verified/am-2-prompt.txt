Illustration style:
Sparse personal independent comic pen drawing, idiosyncratic simple figures, mildly skewed shapes, deliberate imperfection and extreme economy of detail. At least half the picture pure untouched white, including large blank interiors. All subjects share the same informal hand. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, actions, emotions, objects, relative scale, setting and time period. Expressions follow the scene; no forced action, costumes or smiles.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Thin slightly wobbly black pen, uneven pressure, occasional gaps, overshoots and a few searching lines. No polished precision or dramatic brush shadows. Faces few off-center small eyes, simple nose hook and short mouth, individual head shapes and selected age cues. Bodies simplified with slightly awkward personal proportions, natural poses and simple hands. Clothes silhouette plus two fold lines. Machines a few huge loose blocks, simple joints and broad identifying features, empty interiors. Animals few long contours and identifying head marks. Vehicles basic structural shapes, backgrounds abbreviated doodled lines.

Color and shading:
Small muted flat accent palette with black pen and white paper. One perfectly uniform fill per selected area, not color everywhere. Most surfaces white with a few broad pale local-color accents. No shadows, gradients, pigment variation, watercolor bleeding, washes, granulation or material modeling. Preserve specified colors and light through sparse tint hints.

Texture and finish:
Pristine smooth white paper, zero grain or texture overlay. Big blank areas and loose pen do the work. Wear only a few broad irregular plain color patches, no fragments, scratches, speckles or strands, no emblems. Garment patterns barely hinted by a few crossing lines or loose motifs. Trees huge open uneven canopy outlines and sparse trunks, blank interiors. Ground largely white with simple path accent and few grass/rock doodles. Fields few crooked lines. Water flat pale shape with few ripple lines. No leaves, bark, pebbles, detailed grass, dense prints, hatching or realistic textures. Camera and effects separate.
