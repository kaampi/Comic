Illustration style:
Powerful hand-drawn black-ink comic with weighty naturalistically stylized forms, large connected shadows and clean flat color. Sculptural volume through drawing, not rendered realism. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, actions, emotions, objects, relative scale, setting and time period. Expressions follow the scene; no forced action, costumes or smiles. Do not impose superhero musculature.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Every person has conspicuously bold tapered black contours: heavy shoulder, arm and torso strokes narrowing into fine curves, large jaw and neck shadow, grouped black hair and two substantial fold shapes per garment. Faces decisive brow, nose, cheek and jaw planes with selected expression marks; elders retain age. Hands broad readable structural ink. Women and elders as forcefully inked as other subjects. Animals bold outline and large shadow groups. Machines few monumental blocks, simple large joints and broad identifying structures. No bevels, facets, intricate mechanisms or CGI.

Color and shading:
One uniform flat local-color light area and one large solid-black or very dark shadow mass per major form. Shadows large and connected, not fragmented. Highlights few clean shapes, no reflections. Smooth untextured fills, graphic sculptural contrast on every subject. No gradients, airbrush, grain or painted mottling. Preserve specified local colors and lighting.

Texture and finish:
Large clean uninterrupted light planes against black structural shadows. Rust and moss, if specified, only a few large asymmetrical irregular patches with smooth interiors and broadly uneven boundaries. No small fragments, dots, scratches, cracks, grain or plants; no badges or emblems. Forest huge flat canopy groups and connected black shadows, empty interiors. Ground open color plane, sparse grass clumps and plain rocks. Rocks one light plane and one black shadow. Fields broad bands. Water blue and black ribbons with few white glints. Patterns sparse broad lines and large motifs. No grain, fine hatching, screentone, photographic texture or wood grain. Camera and effects separate.
