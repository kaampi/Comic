Illustration style:
Expressive palette-knife painting built from few huge continuous opaque pigment planes. Cinematic clarity through scale, staging and large connected light/shadow masses, not photographic rendering or forced dramatic events. Same broad knife construction across humans, animals, machines and environment. Preserve requested identities, ages, individual builds, species, anatomy, explicit proportions, clothing, poses, emotions, objects, scale, setting, time of day and light sources.

Lines, brushwork and form:
Wide flexible knife dragged flat through pigment: long continuous sweeps, clean scraped-back margins and selected thick ridges, not little raised dabs. Two or three big planes per major form. Faces simple blocked naturalistic planes and chosen accents, hands grouped masses, hair broad drag, clothes few scraped folds and barely suggested patterns. Animals natural proportions, few broad continuous head/body planes and essential harness bands. Machines huge structural planes, simple joints, broad ribs, no intricate seams.

Color and shading:
Restrained slate blues, muted violets, warm ochres, browns and olives following requested local colors and daylight. Light one broad opaque plane, shadow one connected painted mass, sparse selected accents. No gradients, reflected metal, CGI facets or imposed sunset/spotlight. Rust and moss few broad irregular pigment drags rather than detailed surface damage.

Texture and finish:
Opaque dense paint, broad flat-face spreading tracks, long smooth smears and occasional scraped edge. Selected ridges only; substantial calm areas, no all-over impasto mosaic, tiny chips, stipple or grain. Forest huge connected canopy planes and long trunks, no individual leaves. Ground broad earth-color sweeps with few grass/rock masses. Rocks two scraped planes, fields long opaque strips, water few long horizontal knife ribbons. No fine underdrawing, hairs, dense prints, realistic wood grain or photographic underpainting. Camera and effects separate.
